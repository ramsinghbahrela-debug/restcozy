Our Story

Our story

Your home. Your comfort. Your RestCozy.

A home is where busy days slow down, families come together and little moments become lasting memories. At RestCozy, we believe comfort belongs in every corner of that home.

We're beginning with mattresses for different sleeping preferences, needs and budgets. From a softer feel to steadier support, we want to make it easier to find a mattress that feels right for you.

Comfort is in the little details.

The softness of a fabric. The way a mattress supports your body. The feeling of sitting along its edge. These everyday experiences deserve attention, from the centre of your mattress to the corners of your home.

Made for the way Indian homes live.

From a first home to a family bedroom or guest room, every space has different needs. Our aim is to offer clear information about comfort, foam, fabric, sizes and pricing.

Our promise

Thoughtful choices, clear product information and transparent pricing. We're starting with mattresses, with a vision to grow into a home-comfort brand for everyday living.
